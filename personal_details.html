ਕਬਿੱਤ (ka-bit, translation: poem)

This is a personal page, where I explore the beauty of poetry - ਕਬਿਤ. Please avoid this page if you are not interested in poetry, or if you are here by mistake (this page isn't supposed to be available).

ਕਿਤਾਬਾਂ ਵਰਗਿਆਂ ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਖੁਦ ਵਿਚ ਜੋੜ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ 
ਮਿਲੇ ਗਹਿਰਾ ਕਿਤੇ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਾਂ ਵਰਕਾ ਮੋੜ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ
Translation: I connect with people like books. If I find something deep, I fold the page to remember it.
ਕਬਿੱਤ - Kabit - is a specific poetic form, often associated with the writings of Bhai Gurdas.
I am in no way claiming to be a poet, but I do enjoy writing poetry in my free time. I have been writing poetry since I was a child, and it has always been a way for me to express my thoughts and feelings. I find solace in the rhythm and flow of words even when they miss their mark, and I hope that you will too.

This is collection of poems, songs, and quotes that interest me. Some written by me, most are written by others that in some way have moved me. Some of these are in Punjabi, and I can try to provide a translation in English wherever possible.
